Terms & Conditions
Please read and accept before submitting
1. Order process
Submitting this form is an enquiry only, not a confirmed order. Your order is only confirmed once you have received a written quote from us and made payment in full. We do not begin printing until payment has cleared.
2. Image file & soft proof
Once you submit your enquiry you will be asked to send us your image file. We will check it for resolution, colour profile, and crop suitability. For most orders we will send you a digital soft proof showing the crop and composition. Printing will not begin until you have approved the proof in writing via WhatsApp or email.
3. Colour accuracy
We use a colour-calibrated monitor and ICC-profiled printing system to ensure accurate colour reproduction. However, screen colours vary significantly between devices. The soft proof is primarily for approving crop, composition, and wrap placement. Colours on your screen may appear different to the finished print. Our prints are colour-accurate to the sRGB standard. We cannot accept returns based on colour differences between your screen and the printed result once the proof has been approved.
4. Image quality
You are responsible for supplying an image file of sufficient resolution for the size ordered. We will flag any concerns during file review before printing. If you approve printing of a low-resolution file after being warned, we cannot accept a return on quality grounds.
5. Payment
Full payment is required before printing begins. We accept bank transfer and PayPal. Payment details will be sent with your confirmed quote. Prices shown in this form are estimates only. Your confirmed price will be agreed before any payment is requested.
6. Reprints & returns
If a print arrives damaged or contains a confirmed printing error on our part, we will reprint at no charge. We do not offer refunds on custom printed products once production has begun, except where there is a confirmed error by us. Please inspect your order on collection or within 48 hours of delivery and contact us immediately if there is an issue.
7. Turnaround times
Standard turnaround is 3-5 working days from proof approval and cleared payment. Rush orders (24-hour) are subject to availability and carry a 25% surcharge. We will confirm turnaround at the time of order.
8. Your image & data
Your image files are used solely to fulfil your order and are deleted within 30 days of completion unless you ask us to retain them. We do not share your personal data or images with third parties. We are registered with the ICO under GDPR.
9. Copyright
By submitting an image you confirm that you own the copyright or have permission to print it. We accept no liability for copyright infringement arising from images supplied by customers.
